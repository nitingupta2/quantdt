About QDT

Data Analytics • Technical Consulting • Decision Intelligence

Company Overview

Practitioner expertise in data, models, and decisions

Quantitative Data Technologies, LLC is an emerging small business with demonstrated expertise across data science, financial modeling, and geospatial analytics. The firm brings deep practitioner experience in building production-grade analytical systems and interactive visualization platforms.

Headquartered in Fairfax, Virginia, QDT helps government agencies, program offices, and organizations turn complex datasets into actionable decision-support tools — combining sound quantitative methods with reliable, reproducible data engineering.

Our Mission

To give decision-makers trustworthy, transparent analytical insight by pairing rigorous quantitative methods with production-grade data systems and clear, accessible visualization.

Past Performance

Quantitative Data Technologies, LLC is a newly established entity. Our principal brings hands-on experience delivering production analytical systems, financial models, and geospatial visualization platforms.
